# Questions and Answers

## Question 1: Does attendance (percentage) predict overall score?

## Answer: There is a weak positive correlation (r = 0.293) between attendance
## percentage and overall score. There is a slight upward trend as shown
## with the regression line but the wide spread of points suggests
## other influencing factors.


## Question 2: Do students with internet access get a higher overall score
## than students who don’t have internet access?

## Answer: There is no significant difference in overall scores between
## students who do and don’t have internet access. A t-test gave a p-value
## of 0.422, way above the 0.05 threshold. The boxplots support this as they
## appear to be nearly identical. Other variables are very likely to be more
## influential towards overall score.
